Reading this chart: each of the 8 frequency rows is checked twice per time column (two bit-slots per window, labeled a/b) — 16 bit positions total per file, matching the 16-bit payload. The "1" bits (outlined) consistently measure a 12–26dB drop; the "0" bits (unmarked) measure ~0dB, confirming nothing was changed where nothing was supposed to be. The lighter drops in the last two frequency rows reflect less pre-existing energy at those bins in this particular track — a quieter cluster has less headroom to drop before hitting the noise floor.
